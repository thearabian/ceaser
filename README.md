**CEASER by NOXA** is an AI-powered market intelligence and consumer research platform designed to help individuals, entrepreneurs, and businesses make smarter, data-driven decisions.

Powered by an autonomous AI research agent, CEASER investigates markets, analyzes consumer behavior, monitors competitors, identifies emerging trends, and uncovers business opportunities through multi-stage research and evidence-based analysis.

**Key Features:**
- Autonomous AI-powered market research
- Consumer behavior and sentiment analysis
- Competitor intelligence and market monitoring
- Chrome-based web research and data collection
- Multi-stage investigations and source verification
- Market opportunity and trend identification
- Interactive intelligence dashboards
- Evidence-backed reports and actionable insights
- Custom AI API integration

**Mission:** Transform complex market information into clear, reliable, and actionable business intelligence.
